Our Mission

Relieving Suffering, Building Futures

In Love and Faith was established with the belief that no one should face illness, lack of education, or isolation without support. We are a registered charity in England and Wales (No. 1166693), headquartered in Hartlepool.

Our mission centres on the relief of sickness — particularly for those living with HIV/AIDS and other long-term conditions. We also work to bridge educational gaps and facilitate the exchange of care workers between the United Kingdom and developing countries.

We exist to serve a wide range of beneficiaries: children, young people, the elderly, people with disabilities, and the general public — offering grants, human resources, advocacy, advice, and sponsoring vital research.

Our Journey

The Story So Far

From registration to a growing board of trustees, our charity continues to expand its mission and reach.

2016

Charity Founded

In Love and Faith was officially registered with the Charity Commission for England and Wales under charity number 1166693, with a focus on HIV/AIDS relief, education, and general charitable purposes.

2016 – 2024

Building the Foundation

The charity established its operational framework, developing programmes for health relief, educational support, and care worker exchange programmes between the UK and developing countries.

July 2024

Maria-Rosa Matunhike Appointed Trustee

Maria-Rosa Matunhike joined the board as trustee on 15 July 2024, strengthening the charity's governance and leadership.

July 2025

Board Expansion

Dr Joyce Banda and Patience Sibusiwe Chigumira were both appointed as trustees on 1 July 2025, bringing medical expertise and community development experience to the board.

November 2025

Annah Mvundura Joins

Annah Mvundura was appointed as trustee on 26 November 2025, further diversifying the board and expanding the charity's capacity for outreach.

2026

Looking Forward

With a strengthened board and renewed focus, In Love and Faith continues to expand its reach, deepening its impact in HIV/AIDS relief, education, and international care worker exchange programmes.

Our Purpose

Charitable Objectives

As defined in our governing documents and registered with the Charity Commission.

🎗️

Relief of Sickness

Providing relief for people with HIV/AIDS and other long-term health conditions through direct support and grant-making.

📚

Education

Educating the public and people in positions of responsibility regarding HIV/AIDS, and providing pre-school to secondary education for children.

🌐

General Charitable Purposes

Supporting health promotion, disability services, housing, community development, and facilitating care worker exchange programmes.
